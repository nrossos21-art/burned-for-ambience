Before the first euro is spent

Tracking & account setup

The site already fires the standard events through js/tracking.js: page view, view item, view item list, search, add to cart, begin checkout and lead. Pixels load only after the visitor accepts cookies, which keeps you compliant in Cyprus and the EU. UTM parameters and click IDs are stored for 30 days and passed into Shopify checkout so orders are attributed to the right campaign.

StepWhereWhat to do
1. Meta PixelMeta Events Manager → Data sourcesCreate a pixel, paste the ID into metaPixelId. Then in Shopify install the Facebook & Instagram app and turn on the Conversions API so purchases on checkout are sent server-side. Verify the domain burnedforambience.com in Business Settings → Brand Safety.
2. Google Analytics 4analytics.google.comCreate a property, paste the G- ID into ga4Id. Mark purchase, add_to_cart and generate_lead as key events. Link GA4 to Google Ads.
3. Google Ads conversionsGoogle Ads → Goals → ConversionsCreate Purchase (via Shopify's Google & YouTube app, which fires on the thank-you page), Add to cart and Lead. Paste the AW- ID and labels into googleAdsId / googleAdsLabels. Turn on enhanced conversions.
4. Merchant Centermerchants.google.comInstall Shopify's Google & YouTube app so the product feed syncs automatically, or upload feed/products.csv from this folder as a starting feed (129 variants, Google and Meta column names). Set shipping to Cyprus and claim the domain. This unlocks Shopping ads and Performance Max.
5. Meta CatalogCommerce Manager → CatalogsConnect the Shopify catalog through the Facebook & Instagram app (keeps stock in sync) or upload the same CSV. Needed for Advantage+ catalog ads and Instagram product tags.
6. Search Consolesearch.google.com/search-consoleVerify the domain, submit Shopify's sitemap.xml. The site ships Open Graph tags, a Store schema on the homepage and Product schema on every product page, so rich results and link previews work out of the box.
7. Business profilebusiness.google.comCreate or claim the Google Business Profile for the shop with phone +357 99465986, hours 9:00–17:00 and the same photos. Local searches for "candles Cyprus" show it beside the ads.
8. UTM disciplineEvery ad linkUse ?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=refill-q4&utm_content=reel-liftout style links. The site carries them into checkout, and Shopify's order reports then show which creative sold.
9. SpeedPageSpeed InsightsProduct photos are served at 900 px and lazy-loaded; fonts are preconnected. Keep it that way in the Shopify theme: compress uploads, no app bloat, one font family pair.
